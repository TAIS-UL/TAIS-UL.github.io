# TAIS-UL.github.io
Welcome to the TAIS group webpage.

Trustworthy AI for Science (TAIS) is an interdisciplinary research group at the University of Luxembourg led by Prof. Dr. Jun PANG, which aims to advance the frontiers of trustworthy AI and its role in scientific discovery.

The group develops trustworthy, transparent, and responsible AI technologies, while harnessing intelligent systems to accelerate research across domains such as biology, chemistry, and education.
By creating principled methods and powerful AI tools, TAIS enables researchers to generate deeper insights, move faster from data to discovery, and increase the impact and reproducibility of their work.

Our University webpage is https://www.uni.lu/fstm-en/research-groups/trustworthy-ai-for-science/.
